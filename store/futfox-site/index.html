How it works

Five steps, all inside the Web App.

Each step shows the FutFox panel as it looks in the Web App, with an example club.

  1. Sign in to the EA SPORTS FC 27 Web App in Chrome. The FutFox button appears in the bottom-right corner.

  2. Press FutFox: it opens on the Club tab. Press Load my club once per session and FutFox reads your club and your SBC storage the way the Club screen does, in a few seconds.

  3. Open any SBC in the game: FutFox switches to the SBC tab and follows the challenge you're on, Streamlined ones included. Pick another from its list any time and check the rules under Requirements.

  4. Press Find cheapest squad (or Find cheapest cards for a Streamlined SBC). The squad opens on a pitch with its rules on top. Don't want a card? Press Change for another.

  5. FutFox folds into a checklist in the corner. Copy each name, place the card in the game and tick it. Market opens the Transfer Market with the filters set for any card to buy. Then submit it yourself.

FAQ

Questions

Is FutFox free?

Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no ads.

Can it buy cards or submit the SBC for me?

No. FutFox only suggests. It never buys, bids, lists cards, moves players, saves squads or submits an SBC. Every one of those is your own click.

Is it allowed by EA?

EA's terms don't allow unauthorised third-party software. FutFox doesn't automate anything and only reads what the Web App already shows you, but there is still a risk, so use it at your own risk.

What's a Streamlined SBC?

A challenge with a points target instead of a formation: you tick cards in the game's picker until their points reach the target. FutFox asks that picker which cards count and picks the cheapest set, within the options you choose: rating range, most cards, points for now, and fewest coins or fewest cards.

Does it use my SBC storage?

Yes. Load my club also reads your SBC storage, and by default those cards go in first, so the duplicates waiting there get used before your club's cards. Each suggested card says whether it's in your club or SBC storage, so you know which tab to look in.

What does Change do?

It keeps that card out of the suggestion and finds another for the same spot, from your club or SBC storage, or suggests a card to buy if none fits. The rest of the squad stays as it was. "Use them again" brings the cards you kept out back.

Are the coin prices exact?

No. They're estimates based on each card's rating, used to compare squads. FutFox doesn't read market prices, so check the market before you buy.

Where do the Gallery sets come from?

The Web App doesn't share Gallery data, so FutFox publishes the sets itself after each Gallery update. A brand-new set can take a little while to appear in the panel.

Is the Gallery Item Score exact?

For cards in your club and cards you pick, it's the Item Score EA gives that card. A slot that names a version FutFox can't check yet is marked "?", so check that card in the game.

How long does loading my club take?

A few seconds. FutFox reads your club and SBC storage the way the Club screen does, and pauses between pages so it stays gentle with EA's servers. You only need to do it once per session.

I play on PlayStation, Xbox or PC. Does it work for me?

Yes. The Web App shows the same club you play with. FutFox runs in Chrome on a computer, not on a phone or console.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or other browsers?

It's built and tested in Chrome. Other Chromium browsers, such as Edge and Brave, can load it the same way, but they aren't tested.

How do I update it?

If you installed it yourself, download the latest futfox.zip again, replace the futfox folder and press the reload icon on FutFox in chrome://extensions. The Chrome Web Store version will update itself.

Is FutFox in Arabic?

Yes. The panel speaks Arabic when the Web App is in Arabic, and Options has a switch for English or العربية.

What does it collect?

Nothing about you. There's no account and no tracking. Once a day FutFox downloads the public list of Gallery sets, sending nothing with the request. Your options, the cards it has seen and your picks stay in your own browser. Read the privacy policy.
