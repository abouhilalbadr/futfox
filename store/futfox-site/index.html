How it works

Five steps, all inside the Web App

These are the real FutFox panel screens, shown with an example club.

  1. Sign in to the EA SPORTS FC 27 Web App in Chrome. The FutFox button appears in the bottom-right corner.

  2. Open an SBC set so the Web App loads its requirements, then press FutFox. Pick the challenge and check its rules under Requirements.

  3. Press Load my club once per session. FutFox reads your club list the way the Club screen does, one page every few seconds.

  4. Press Find cheapest squad. You get the rating, chemistry and estimated coins, and each player is marked Untradeable or Club. By default your active squad and players rated 87 or more stay out.

  5. Search market opens the Transfer Market with quality, position, league and nation already set. There's no rating filter there, so look for the rating FutFox shows. You press Search and Buy, then build and submit the squad in the SBC screen.

Safety

It looks. You decide.

FutFox only reads and suggests. Every coin you spend and every squad you submit is your own click.

FutFox does

  • Reads the SBC's requirements
  • Reads your club, only when you press Load my club
  • Suggests the cheapest squad that meets the rules
  • Fills in the market search for cards you're missing

Only you do

  • Buy and bid on cards
  • List your cards on the market
  • Move players and save squads
  • Submit the SBC

FAQ

Questions

Is FutFox free?

Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no ads.

Can it buy cards or submit the SBC for me?

No. FutFox only suggests. It never buys, bids, lists cards, moves players, saves squads or submits an SBC. Every one of those is your own click.

Is it allowed by EA?

EA's terms don't allow unauthorised third-party software. FutFox doesn't automate anything and only reads what the Web App already shows you, but there is still a risk, so use it at your own risk.

Are the coin prices exact?

No. They're estimates based on each card's rating, used to compare squads. FutFox doesn't read market prices, so check the market before you buy.

Why does loading my club take a while?

FutFox reads your club one page every few seconds, the way the Club screen does, so it stays gentle with EA's servers. You only need to do it once per session.

I play on PlayStation, Xbox or PC. Does it work for me?

Yes. The Web App shows the same club you play with. FutFox runs in Chrome on a computer, not on a phone or console.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or other browsers?

It's built and tested in Chrome. Other Chromium browsers, such as Edge and Brave, can load it the same way, but they aren't tested.

How do I update it?

If you installed it yourself, download the code again, replace the futfox-main folder and press the reload icon on FutFox in chrome://extensions. The Chrome Web Store version will update itself.

What does it collect?

Nothing. There's no FutFox server. Your panel options are saved in your own browser. Read the privacy policy.
