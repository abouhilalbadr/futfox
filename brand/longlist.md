# FutCraft concept longlist

Scores 1–5: idea clarity · distinction · simplicity · relevance · 16 px strength. Max 25.

Mark-type guidance (skill §12): the name is short and new, so a wordmark-led combination fits. The icon lives mostly as
a 16 px toolbar icon, so a compact letterform or symbol comes first. Explore three different types.

Trademark note: no F+C monogram. "FC" reads as EA SPORTS FC, EA's own brand. The earlier "Carved tile" (F and C
locking together) is dropped for this reason too.
