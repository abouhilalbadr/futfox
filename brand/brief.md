# FutFox logo brief

**Name:** FutFox (one word, capital F twice). Renamed from FutCraft once the fox mascot was chosen.
**What it is:** a free, read-only Chrome extension for the EA SPORTS FC 27 Ultimate Team Web App. It finds the
cheapest way to complete an SBC from your own club and pre-fills market searches for the rest. It never buys or submits.
**Audience:** Ultimate Team players who grind SBCs, 16–35, used to FUTBIN, FUT.GG, FUTWIZ and in-game UI.
**Adjectives:** clever, precise, trustworthy, crafty (in the maker sense), a little playful.
**Promise:** every piece fits, at the lowest cost.
**Competitors:** FUTBIN, FUT.GG, FUTWIZ, FUTGenie, FC Helper, FUT Evolution.
**Constraints:** must work as a 16 px Chrome toolbar icon and a 128 px store icon; one colour; dark UI first.
Must not use EA marks, the FUT card shield silhouette, EA fonts or card art.

## Category clichés to avoid (or twist)
Football ball, shield or crest, the gold card silhouette, lightning bolts, stars, trophies, pitch lines, the letters
"FUT" set in a sporty italic.

## Word map
- craft → forge, anvil, chisel, tiles, bricks, joinery, pieces that click
- SBC → squad, puzzle, eleven slots, requirements, chemistry links, "fodder" and "bricks"
- formation → dots and links on a tactics board
- promise → fits, cheapest, exact

**Intersections:** tiles that click into a whole; a pitch assembled from parts; the maker's tools meeting football.

**Direction (owner's call):** no letters in the symbol. The name lives in the wordmark beside it.

## Assumptions
No existing colours or equity. Shelf test: FUT.GG is orange on black and FUTGenie is gold on black, so warm accents
blend in. Chosen: deep navy #0B1324 with chemistry mint #34E3A0 (the colour of a full chemistry link). Wordmark: Inter Bold
(SIL Open Font Licence), outlined.

## Round 3 outcome (25 Oct 2026)
Owner feedback on rounds 1–2: too plain, not gamer enough, wrong colours, no letters. Direction: a fox mascot.
Chosen: **Bandit**, a fox in a bandit mask, Fox Violet #8B5CF6 with Chem Lime #C6FF3D eyes on Night #0E0B1F.
Rejected: low-poly/orange fox (MetaMask, Firefox, GitLab), fox profile (read as a wolf), winking fox (whiskers fill in
at 16 px). Final files and rules: `logo/GUIDE.md`.
